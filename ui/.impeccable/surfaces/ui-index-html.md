Scope: the Studio view, home of the Magic CAD UI. Experience mode. Direction pinned by BRIEF_STUDIO.md; no concept roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The object owns the screen. Refuses the admin dashboard of tables that the user rejected, and refuses dev-tool viewer chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Flat warm off-white workbench, a light-grey matte filament part with a baked contact shadow, floating translucent hairline panes, one humanist system sans, safety orange only on Build, its rising cut face, and problems.

STORY: The visitor sees the part, turns it in their hand, presses Build and watches it rise while the real gates light, then reads the verdict in plain words. They understand what was verified, and that a replay is not a build.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Part centred at 75 % of height, slowly turning. Wordmark, switcher and Mission control across the top without a surface. Details panel top right. Iso/Front/Top/Side chips and the orange Build button centred at the bottom.

FORM: Product photograph with camera-app controls, from the brief, position 1. Signature interaction: the rising cut, an orange cross-section climbing through the part. seed: none (brief-pinned, no roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
